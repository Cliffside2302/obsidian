Due to crime rising in the lower slums, [[Rapture]] has created a police force. They aid to keep [[Splicers]] in check and to minimize smuggling operations with their detectives going undercover and investigating. Like any other government agency, corruption is not unheard of. No honour among thieves? The notorious Gang of bandits is still around, destroying homes of proper citizens; help detective to find them and he’ll surely owe you one.
