Sword is a powerful melee weapon that can be drenched in [[Adam]] and given special status effects with [[Plasmid]]s. Burning sword or electrifying lightning striking menace. [[Telekinesis]] improves block ability and delivers even heavier strikes on the target. It can also be thrown and recalled using [[Telekinesis]]. With the [[Houdini]] Plasmid you can let it teleport back to you. An icy sword with [[Winter Blast]] may also be of some use...
