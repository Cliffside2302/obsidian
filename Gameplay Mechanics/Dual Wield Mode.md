Through [[Plasmid Ads]] and [[Public service announcements]], the player gets new ideas for plasmid and weapon combos. Plasmids with primary/alternate Fire Mode and can be equipped in an unlock-able [[Dual Wield Mode]] which allows the player to wield 2 Plasmids at once instead of a [[Weapon]], and a [[Plasmid]]. This induces new combinations for [[Elemental Effects]]. For example lightning imbued fire or icicles that are formed by [[Winter Blast]] and thrown with [[Telekinesis]] as a deadly form of silent, ranged combat.
