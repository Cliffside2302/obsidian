Citizens of Rapture, succumbed to the cancer like cell growth side effects of Adam have been given the name Splicers. Their appearance varies from looking like ordinary men up to almost not identifiable as human anymore. They roam the districts of [[Rapture]] in search of [[Adam]] that they have developed an addiction for due to repeated use. Splicers can form alliances and roam in Packs increasing their chances of survival and overcoming even bigger foes. Some even run a form of Trade on the [[Black Market]], but be careful: never trust a splicer, their only goal is to get more Adam.
